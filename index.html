Why does plastic pollution matter?

Have you ever seen a plastic bottle on the sidewalk or near a river? It might look like a small thing, but plastic can stay in the environment for a very long time.

Animals can mistake plastic for food, and litter can make neighborhoods and waterways less clean. People can help by using fewer single-use items and throwing waste away properly.

What is single-use plastic?

It's plastic that we use once and then throw away, like a shopping bag, straw, or takeout container. A reusable item can be a simple alternative.

Everyday examples

You don't need to change your whole life at once. Here are a few things that are pretty easy to try:

  • Bring a reusable water bottle.
  • Use a tote bag when you go shopping.
  • Skip plastic cutlery when you don't need it.
  • Reuse a clean container instead of throwing it away right away.
